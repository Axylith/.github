# Code of Conduct

The Axylith organization is committed to providing a productive, professional environment for everyone contributing to or interacting with our projects.

## The short version

Be respectful. Be direct. Disagree about ideas, not people. Don't harass anyone. Don't waste people's time.

If you're not sure whether something is appropriate, it probably isn't.

## What's expected

- **Treat people with respect**, including people whose work you disagree with.
- **Focus on technical merit.** Critique code, designs, and arguments — not the people behind them.
- **Be direct.** Pushback is welcome; dancing around the point wastes everyone's time.
- **Assume good faith.** Most miscommunication is misunderstanding, not malice.
- **Disclose AI assistance.** If you used an LLM to write code or text being submitted, note it.
- **Respect maintainer time.** Search before opening duplicate issues. Provide reproduction steps. Don't repeatedly bump issues.

## What's not tolerated

- Personal attacks, insults, threats
- Harassment, including targeted unwanted contact
- Discriminatory language or behavior based on race, gender, sexuality, religion, nationality, disability, or other personal characteristics
- Doxxing or publishing private information without consent
- Sexual content in project spaces
- Sustained disruption of discussions or work
- Bad-faith arguments, sealioning, "just asking questions" framing of harassment
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or low-effort AI-generated submissions

## Enforcement

Maintainers will warn for first violations, except in cases severe enough to warrant immediate removal. Repeated violations result in temporary or permanent bans from organization spaces (GitHub, Discord, email lists).

To report a violation:

- **Email:** `conduct@axylith.com`
- **DM a maintainer** if email isn't an option

Reports are confidential. We will not retaliate against good-faith reporters.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Axylith GitHub repositories (issues, PRs, discussions, code comments)
- The Axylith Discord server
- Official Axylith communication channels (email, social media)
- In-person events organized by Axylith (when they exist)

It also applies to behavior outside these spaces that affects the safety of the community within them.

## Acknowledgements

This policy draws from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), without the heavier infrastructure of formal escalation paths that small projects don't need.
